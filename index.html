Headline patterns

OAuth is the default49 apps use OAuth2 as the primary auth label; 13 also use OAuth in a mixed auth label. PAT/Bearer and API-key paths are the next common patterns.
Easy wins are concentratedDeveloper, productivity, and CRM-style apps offer the highest proportion of documented REST plus self-serve setup. Start with these before regulated or enterprise sales apps.
The blocker is access, not APIs31 apps are gated. 17 also lacked enough public, stable integration coverage in the evidence pass. Human outreach is mainly needed for approvals and partner terms.
